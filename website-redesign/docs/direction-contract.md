## Direction contract

### THESIS
Graffiti's opening makes the brand itself the poster: the exact logo sits beside a huge GRAFFITI wordmark rotated 90 degrees to the left, drawn as a hollow yellow-gold outline with a faint registration echo and a restrained pointer trace. The typographic poster establishes the idea; six real recordings provide product proof below it.

### OWN-WORLD
The ground is an emissive graphite void (`#0c0d0c`) with warm ivory (`#f7efd9`) and the logo's antique gold (`#d6aa4a`) carrying the primary signal. A restrained coral signal (`#ef634d`) marks live states. Typography is a condensed, heavy sans for display and a compact system monospace for telemetry. Panels are hard-edged, offset, and visibly structural; no glass, blur, rounded cards, or soft shadows.

### STORY
The visitor understands that their wallpaper becomes responsive, explores six real effects in the requested sequence, discovers Gallery's custom-shape and image/color fills, previews the existing 3D model, and reaches download and feedback states.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
The header is a narrow signal rail. The exact Graffiti logo is oversized on the left, with “A product of Klaav” and the headline below. On the right, a huge GRAFFITI wordmark is rotated 90 degrees to the left and drawn with a yellow-gold outline only; the interior remains the void color. A reticle, four distinct geometric markers, and a coral trace provide the pointer response. The entire poster links to Effects. Touch and reduced-motion versions remain static. No wallpaper photo, local-account badge, solid wordmark, caption, or oversized pointer sculpture appears here.

The hero is height-aware: the entire headline and every wordmark letter must be visible at scroll position zero, including shorter laptop viewports. On mobile, the wordmark shares the first row with the logo and headline instead of following the supporting copy. Its wrapper follows intrinsic text width to prevent font-dependent clipping. Hero metadata omits the Wallpaper label.

### FORM
Retro-futurist brutalism, seed key `5c5c3b9f`. Effects use a live stage and vertical queue, with the stage header identifying the active effect. The third feature is named Gallery in navigation, headings, accessibility labels, and source names. Its copy explains drawing shapes and filling them with images or solid colors. Download is a logo-gold utility field. Feedback uses category buttons plus a local feedback viewer and small hard-offset geometric tiles. Web Wallpaper copy explains embedded WebView rendering and its potential RAM/GPU cost. Actions use smooth event-driven motion rather than continuous animation.

Gallery retains its visual composition and animation phases; only the scroll travel is shortened by 30% (250vh → 175vh on desktop, 180vh → 126vh on smaller screens).

### FINISH
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
