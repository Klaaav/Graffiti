### THESIS
Graffiti's opening makes pointer movement the subject: the exact logo sits beside an original dimensional cursor illustration. The illustration establishes the idea; six real recordings provide product proof below it.

### OWN-WORLD
The ground is an emissive graphite void (`#0c0d0c`) with warm ivory (`#f7efd9`) and the logo's antique gold (`#d6aa4a`) carrying the primary signal. A restrained coral signal (`#ef634d`) marks live states. Typography is a condensed, heavy sans for display and a compact system monospace for telemetry. Panels are hard-edged, offset, and visibly structural; no glass, blur, rounded cards, or soft shadows.

### STORY
